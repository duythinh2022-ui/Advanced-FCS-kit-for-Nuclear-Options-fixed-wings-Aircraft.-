Base: **2.6.3**. One change: the trade is a split again. Bench-only, not flown. The push-over from a post-stall rotation at 115-135 kt is analysed below and **not** changed.

## What the 22:17 log said (2.6.3)
- Corkscrews over ~300 kt: the hitch is gone (no `yawSatPitch` on the 303-321 kt entries except where sideslip was going past 4).
- Corkscrews at 174-210 kt: roll target 30-35 deg/s against `pSmax` 48-81, pitch at the full 60 deg/s ask. The trade (`pullTrade` 35-49) was cutting the roll to its 30 deg/s floor and nothing touched the pitch. Your "the slower it is, the more pitch and less roll". The reason: since 2.5.1 `PullRollTrade` 0.7 moved the roll limit 70 % of the way to the trade's target and the other 30 % of the shortfall was simply uncovered (the 2.2.7 governor had covered it with its AoA-rise hold at the margin); at low IAS the yaw authority is small, so the roll target sat at the floor and the pitch ran free.

## Change: the pitch covers what the roll's share leaves
The trade sizes the roll rate at which the sweep's yaw demand (pS cos AoA x AoA rate) fits the rudders' free yaw; that is a ratio R of the roll on hand. The roll gives up `PullRollTrade` of the way (as before); the pitch now gives up the rest: the AoA-rate ask is held to R / R_roll, so the pair's demand lands on the share with the split you set. 1 = all roll (2.5.1-2.6.3), 0 = all pitch, **0.7 = 70 / 30**. It acts in the same block as the pull cap (`capPull` / `capSweep`, either AoA sign), through the same `yawSatPitch` path, with the same floor; telemetry `tradeAlpha` (the AoA-rate cap in force, deg/s) after `paceRel`. Nothing else moves: the rate cap, window, margin, probe, the 2.6.3 forecast all as they were.
