# F-22E FCS 2.5.4

Base: **2.5.3**. Two fixes from the first 2.5.3 flight (31 Oct 09:44 log, 341 s, level 3, margins 4/5/5). Bench-only beyond what that log shows.

## What the 09:44 log said about 2.5.3
- **The moderate-AoA stops and reversals are fixed.** All 23 stops / reversals above 5 deg AoA and 100 m/s: mean peak sideslip 2.9, worst 6.9 (a reversal at 38 deg AoA, 200 s), reversals mean 3.2 - against 4.8 / 14.2 / 5.7 at level 3 and 3.1 / 8.5 / 4.8 at level 1 in the 01:40 log. Reversals from 117-161 deg/s at 250 km/h: 0.8-1.4 deg. 16 excursions past 5 deg in 328 s airborne (01:40 log: 33 in 311 s).
- **Rates are smooth.** 0.3 s detrended p/q/r residual over the flight 2.4 / 0.9 / 0.3 deg/s rms (01:40: 2.2 / 1.05 / 0.32; 00:50: 2.0 / 0.95 / 0.36); seconds with pitch residual > 3 deg/s 0.9 % (1.6 / 0.6 %); yaw never > 2; no rate-reversal events above thresholds. The negative-AoA corkscrew start (325 s, -30 deg AoA, 111 m/s, 50 deg/s) is the cleanest entry in the log (p/q/r residual 0.2 / 0.8 / 0.1, tracking errors 1-5 deg/s); the push-overs under roll (248 / 273 / 311 / 323 / 334 s) and pull-throughs (280 / 317 / 328 s) show rate residuals of the same size as positive-AoA entries. What was not smooth is the roll-rate *target*: it stepped by more than 50 deg/s 1-7 times inside each crossing (0 <-> 133 at 324 s while the rudders swung through center), which the jet followed as a hitch in the roll.
- **What remains is the AoA sweep through zero under a roll:** pull-throughs from negative AoA with full roll 10.8-15.2 deg (280, 317, 328 s), push-overs from positive to negative with 75-140 deg/s of roll 6.6-9.3 (249, 312, 324, 334 s), and the deep stall under 50 m/s IAS at 65 deg AoA (87 s 7.4, 148-152 s 27.0 - the one past 10; `aYaw` ~8 deg/s^2, nothing governs there).

## The two 2.5.3 bugs and the fixes
1. **The yaw window at the zero crossing.** It divided by sin(AoA) with a 0.03 floor; within +-3 deg of zero AoA a leftover yaw rate became a nonsense roll demand - it *raised* the roll target above the stick (378-571 deg/s; 15.6 s of the flight, mostly at 40-85 m/s, half of it above 40 deg AoA) and flipped it to zero as the AoA crossed, chattering with the rudders' usage. Now: the window is **off within +-sin 0.05 (~3 deg) of zero AoA** (the roll's share of the yaw demand is too small for any roll to settle a yaw error there - the yaw loop owns it); it **may hold the roll in progress** while the yaw comes down (that is the paced brake / reversal) but **never asks for more roll than the stick or the roll in progress, and never for a roll against it**; and its gate releases over 0.3 s (`PaceGateTau`) instead of dropping to zero for the ticks the rudders pass through center.
2. **The AoA-sweep pitch cap sized itself from the roll reference**, and only with the roll target at its cap. When the window dropped the reference to zero the body roll was still 70-120 deg/s for half a second and the cap let the push / pull run at 50-60 deg/s of AoA rate over it (324 / 328 s); with the stick released mid-roll (280 s) the cap was off altogether. Now it is sized from the **larger of the reference and the measured velocity-vector roll** and applies whenever a roll is in progress (`|pS| > 5`), stick held or not.

## Bench
- Stop / reversal sweep (32 cases, 450-850 km/h, 5-23 deg AoA): identical to 2.5.3 (L3 worst stop / reversal 3.2 / 3.9, mean 2.1 / 2.6, roll 108; L1 3.4 / 4.9). The fixes act only around zero AoA.
- Rolling pulls, reversals, 65 deg release, 1 g reversal: identical to 2.5.3 (notes 2.5.3). Push + roll 300 km/h 9.9 -> 9.5, 200 km/h 3.9 -> 3.6.
- Log replays of the 09:44 sweeps (pilot's sticks; bench vs game): the bench **over-reads negative-AoA cases badly** (82 s 14.9 vs 7.7 in game; 312 s 27.9 vs 8.5; 334 s 14.0 vs 6.6) - its dihedral / yaw at negative AoA is not calibrated, so these are directional only. 2.5.3 -> 2.5.4: 82 s 14.9 -> 10.1, 249 s 14.9 -> 12.4, 312 s 27.9 -> 21.3, 334 s 14.0 -> 11.6, 324 s 7.3 -> 8.4, 328 s 15.1 -> 16.5, 200 s 4.8 -> 6.5 (game 6.9). Target inflation 0 in every replay (2.5.3: 2-34 samples), target steps > 50 deg/s 0-1 per event (2.5.3: 1-6).
- New bench scenarios (push-over under roll from a pull, pull-through from -25 deg with roll, roll released at the crossing) run the same on 2.5.3 and 2.5.4; one of them (full push 1.5 s + full roll from +8 at 350 km/h) departs to 40 deg on **both** benches while the game's equivalents stayed at 6.6-9.3 - another sign the bench's negative-AoA plant is off. **Negative-AoA calibration of the bench is the open item before more work on this corner.**

## Not verified
- Not flown. The pull-through and push-over sideslips (7-15 deg in the 09:44 log) are what to watch; the bench cannot predict them yet.
- `PaceGateTau` 0.3 s and the +-3 deg skip are chosen, not fitted.
