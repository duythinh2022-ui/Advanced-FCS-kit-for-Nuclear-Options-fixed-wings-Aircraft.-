## Method
- `bench parts` (full `Plant.Forces`) at each AoA 0-50 deg, 110 m/s, rho 1.2, stabs / flaperons / rudders 0, no thrust, slats on schedule, no rates. NO has no Mach effect on lift, so the margin does not depend on speed; pitch accelerations scale with dynamic pressure (these are at about 7.3 kPa, ~400 km/h IAS).
- Margin = neutral point aft of CG = -dM/dN, central difference over +-1 deg. Positive = stable. Iyy 307,237 kg m^2.
- Two frames: **model** = the game's aero as written at the nominal CG (`MisStab=1 MisRud=1 MisWing=1 MisBody=1 MisFin=1 MisAil=1 MisFlap=1 CgAftShift=0`); **log-calibrated** = bench defaults (31 Oct fit: CG 15.5 cm forward, stab lift x0.72, wing/body x0.95).
- Two-zone: `TwoZoneAirframe` with refit 0.25 (blended schedules), `StabOutFrac` 0.5, plate curve dip 0.9 / width 12, slat coupling (no effect at zero deviation), fins unblanked.
- **Bench tool fix:** `parts` built its TOTAL from the per-part dump, which leaves out the two-zone stab's outboard-zone force. A `FORCES` line (whole-aircraft F, M from `Plant.Forces`) was added in this session's bench copy only. At 10 deg the outboard zone is worth -16.8 deg/s^2 of pitch. Dynamic runs were never affected. With the fix, `parts` and `cmsweep` agree within 0.03 m at low AoA for the two-zone airframe. (Kit 5.0: use `cmsweep`, which includes the outboard zone.)
